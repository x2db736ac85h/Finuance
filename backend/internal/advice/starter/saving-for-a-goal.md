title: Saving for something with a deadline
tags: goals, saving, planning
---
A holiday in summer, a new laptop, a deposit: saving for a known amount by a known date is easier when the monthly amount is worked out once and then automated.

**Work out the monthly amount.** Divide what you still need by the months left. 3,000 by June, with six months to go, is 500 a month. If that is more than you can set aside, choose: a later date, a smaller target, or less spending somewhere else.

**Make it visible.** A savings goal shows what you have put aside against a steady pace. Being a little behind early on is fine; being far behind with two months left is the moment to adjust.

**Keep it apart.** A separate account (or sub-account) for each bigger goal stops the money from quietly paying for everyday things. A goal can follow one account, so its progress is that account's growth.

**Find the money.** Pick one or two categories where you spend more than you would like, and set a goal to spend a percentage less on them than in the months before. The difference goes to the goal.

**When it is done,** close the goal and redirect the monthly amount to the next one or to your emergency fund before it gets absorbed by spending.
