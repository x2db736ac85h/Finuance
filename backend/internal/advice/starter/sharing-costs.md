title: Sharing costs fairly with friends and partners
tags: sharing, groups, households
---
Shared costs go wrong less often over money than over unclear expectations. Agree on the rules before the first bill.

**Choose how to split.**

- **Equally**: simplest, and fair when everyone uses about the same.
- **By shares**: for different usage or incomes, for example 60/40 for a couple with different salaries, or by nights stayed on a trip.
- **Exact amounts**: when people ordered different things.

The method can differ per expense; what matters is that everyone knows which one applies.

**Record expenses as they happen.** In a shared group, each person adds what they paid, and the balances show who owes whom. Settling up with the fewest transfers is easier than everyone paying everyone.

**Settle regularly.** For a trip, settle at the end. For a household, settle monthly, before the amounts grow big enough to be awkward.

**Keep personal and shared apart.** Your own accounts and categories stay private; a shared group holds only copies of what you chose to share.

**For couples,** a common approach is a joint pot for shared bills, each paying in a fixed amount or a share of income, with personal spending kept separate.

_General information, not financial advice: your situation may differ. For decisions with tax or legal consequences, ask a qualified adviser._
