title: Keeping subscriptions under control

Subscriptions are easy to start and easy to forget. Each one is small; together they can be one of the largest lines in a budget.

**Find them all.** Recurring payments are found in your transactions: monthly, weekly and yearly charges from the same payee with a similar amount. Yearly ones are the easiest to forget, because they appear once and renew quietly.

**Ask three questions of each.**

1. Did I use it last month?
2. Would I sign up again today at this price?
3. Do I pay for the same thing twice (two music services, two cloud storage plans)?

If the answer to either of the first two is no, cancel it or set a reminder for the week before it renews.

**Watch for creep.** Prices go up a little every year, and free trials turn into paid plans. Once a quarter, compare the recurring payments with the list from last time.

**Share where it is allowed.** Family plans split between people who live together are often cheaper per person. A shared group keeps track of who paid and who owes what.

**Count the yearly cost.** A monthly charge times twelve is a better number to judge it by: 15 a month is 180 a year.
